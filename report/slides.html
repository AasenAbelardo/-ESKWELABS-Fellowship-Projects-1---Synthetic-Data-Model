How a concentration is made

A baseline, a commute, a season, and a memory.

Traffic

Bells at 08:00 and 18:30. Saturday is quieter, Sunday quieter still, and a holiday cuts the wave to 0.55.

Dry season

A cosine that peaks in early February. January through April is the dirty block on every roadside row.

Rain and wind

Rain washes particles out. Wind disperses them, then flattens. A typhoon is not allowed to hit the instrument floor.

Memory

Two autoregressive shocks, one shared by the airshed and one local, so a dirty hour is usually followed by another.

Ozone does the opposite of the traffic gases. It rises toward 14:00, and it falls wherever fresh NO2 is high.

One year, two sites

Same calendar. Not the same color.

EDSA’s median reporting day is 36 µg/m³, and 53% of those days sit above moderate. Antipolo’s median day is 11. Grey is a frozen sensor. Teal is the typhoon.

Where the year was dirtiest

The roads and the plants. Then a long drop to the ridge.

Valenzuela 39, EDSA 38, Antipolo 12 µg/m³. Larger markers opened more episodes. Antipolo and Las Piñas open none.

The shape of a day

Commute, Sunday, afternoon.

Sunday roadside NO2 falls from 58 to 39 µg/m³. Ozone at 14:00 is 94 against 29 at 06:00, and it opposes NO2 at r = -0.64.

The dry season

January through April, on every roadside row.

The wet months cool the grid. August at EDSA is only the hours the sensor was actually reporting.

What clears the basin

Rain first. Wind second. Then wind stops helping.

A dry day sits near 32 µg/m³. A day with 40 mm or more sits near 9. Daily r with rain is -0.18, with wind -0.46.

Read it with the flag on

Three ways to lie with this file.

01

Call it DENR data. It is a synthetic airshed with landmark coordinates.

02

Call the index a legal AQI. The breakpoints are the US EPA table, on trailing means, not DENR’s.

03

Average August at EDSA without the quality flag. The frozen week becomes a clean week that never happened.

Firework nights look like combustion. The Valenzuela job inflates PM10 only. Both are features.
